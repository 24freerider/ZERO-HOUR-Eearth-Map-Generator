ZERO HOUR EARTH - changelog
===========================
Versioning: 0.X.Y - X = new feature / bigger change, Y = bug fix.
Versions 0.1 - 0.8 were numbered afterwards (the program used to be called "CNC Earth").

v0.53.7
- Battle Royale now creates the starter before clearing the original base. The starter has 4x HP and there are 25% more street rebels where space allows.

v0.53.6
- Added Battle Royale for 2–32 slots: one rifleman, street rebels, four salvage vehicles and five radioactive zone stages.

v0.53.5
- Restored the v0.52.1 Continuous Zombie spawn rate, unit mix and boss schedule. The phase counter keeps advancing and the active zombie limit remains configurable.

v0.53.4
- Restored the previous road and junction rendering, with yellow lines on side streets and white dashed lines on major roads.

v0.53.3
- Invasion ground squads enter near the map edges with random timing, direction and group sizes.
- Ground squads seek hostile units and buildings along the way.

v0.53.2
- Junctions keep the main road continuous and connect side roads with short unmarked sections.
- Roundabouts use a closed loop and residential streets use narrower, unmarked asphalt.
- Roads and tracks respect gaps in OSM geometry. Dirt roads use their own surface without additional ground paint.

v0.53.1
- Invasion attacks run through the last start's base GLA AI, like Zombie.
- Residential and access roads are rendered as native roads again, with dashed white markings.

v0.53.0
- Invasion: co-op defence of one shared headquarters, hidden starting command centres, and one builder per defender.
- Configurable preparation time (default three minutes) and escalating attacks from a selected faction.

v0.52.4
- Main roads use white dashed markings. Residential streets and access lanes use plain asphalt.
- OSM road surfaces distinguish cobbles, concrete, gravel, dirt and rutted tracks.
- Parking bays use ParkingLines strips, with cars positioned in the same rows.

v0.52.3
- Standard skirmish prepares level build pads and protects approaches to starting supplies in automatic and manual layouts.
- Town → Advanced exposes small-building density, the fallback floor count and tall-building stacking.

v0.52.2
- Adjust the settings panel using its divider.
- Continuous mode has an editable active-zombie cap (default 180), shown during play.
- Continuous phases continue past level 40, with faster spawns and more high-tier zombies when tiers are enabled.

v0.52.1
- Respawn Ambush replaces the Survival and Defense paradrop. After losing your shop vehicle, use the sidebar ability to place a Humvee instantly on open ground.
- Respawn works in solo Survival. In co-op, a full team wipe still ends the match.

v0.52.0
- Resizable settings panel, compact navigation and editable numbers beside sliders.
- West–east map shapes (2:1, 3:1, 4:1) and a north-vs-south start layout.
- Manual starts show base boundaries. The generator finds, levels and clears nearby dry build pads for the AI.
- Landscape adds rocks, stumps and bushes along banks, in forests and on slopes.
- Classic Zombie without Clearance spawns from a central tunnel. Choose waves or continuous spawns with rising difficulty.
- Survival and Defense expose the Humvee airdrop in the sidebar and keep its source under player control through delivery.

v0.51.2 - internal: generator.py split into smaller files
- The generator's behaviour is unchanged (verified: the same map byte for byte before and after the split, in 6 modes and on the real Poltar data).
- generator.py (4900 lines) is now the core plus the modules gconst.py (constants), g_terrain, g_roads, g_buildings, g_bases, g_nature, g_insurgents, g_zini, g_zmode, g_output. Every further change touches just one smaller file.

v0.51.1 - overpasses and manual stacking of blocks of flats
- Overpasses: when two roads cross without a shared junction and one of them is tagged as a bridge in OSM (bridge / layer), the upper road becomes a real bridge. The ground round the upper road is raised to deck height (an embankment with gentle ramps); only a strip along the lower road (also a motorway with both carriageways, or a railway) stays at road level, so units can drive under the bridge. The bridge type follows the upper road (four-lane, two-lane, cobblestone, wooden). Crossings without a bridge tag are left as they were.
- Editor: new field "Stack: extra floors (0-6)" and quick buttons Stack +1 / +2 / +3 / = 0. The same model is put on top of the selected block of flats (several at once too). Works on AsiaApartment02 and AsianApartmentSingle01 (not on CivilianHighrise02). The value is saved with the map's edits and used every time the map is generated again.

v0.51.0 – setting up the map before generating
- Pre-check above the Generate button: whether the OSM data is already cached or has to be downloaded, number of buildings in the area (after the quick preview), warnings (too many streets for the 4000 road-segment limit, overlapping or too large bases, missing INIZH.big, a map with this name already exists)
- Quick OSM preview (Area page): roads, water, forests and building density drawn over the map picker before generating (areas up to 8 km)
- The automatic start layout (corners, circle, two teams, Clearance, Zombie Survival ...) is drawn on the map with the base squares of "Base space"; it uses the same code as the generator (preflight.layout_rel)
- A map with the same name no longer gets overwritten silently: the program asks – overwrite, or create a new one (Name_2, Name_3 ...)
- Setting profiles (Export page): save the whole setup including the place and manual starts under a name; four built-in templates (normal skirmish, Clearance, Zombie Survival, Zombie Defense)
- Search shows several candidates to pick from; the ★ button next to the search box keeps favourite and recently used places
- Random button next to the seed
- Settings are saved automatically a moment after every change (not only when closing or generating); the open/closed state of the log is now remembered too

v0.44.1  -  Satellite buildings only for your area
* Satellite buildings are fetched only for the map's area: from one world file (Google + Microsoft + OSM, VIDA /
  source.coop, PMTiles format) only the tiles over the map are read - usually 1 - 10 MB.
* Fallback if that fails: the Microsoft tile (at most 100 MB); the program keeps only your area and deletes the big file
  at once. The result for the area is saved (a few hundred kB), the next run downloads nothing.

v0.44.0  -  Buildings from satellite images, smooth roundabouts, simpler terrain
* NEW: when OpenStreetMap has fewer than 30 buildings in the area (Iraq, many places in Africa and Asia), the program
  adds buildings from satellite images automatically (Microsoft Global ML Building Footprints). Only the needed tile is
  downloaded and kept in ~/.cncearth/msbuildings, so the next run needs no download.
* NEW: roundabouts are a smooth circle of one radius (from OSM junction=roundabout), big enough for the road, and every
  entry joins exactly onto the circle - no more "star of planks".
* The terrain is always real, from elevation data (hills 1:1) - the terrain settings are gone.
* The map name is on the Export page.
* Target game GeneralsX: if the road limit in GeneralsX is not raised yet, the program raises it while generating (only
  in the GeneralsX folder) - full detail with footpaths and every street.
* The summary above the button also shows the target game, detail, road limit and what goes on the map.
* ZombieSurvival Defense has its own starting money (default 5000), Survival keeps its own.
